Ovis beta

Try Ovis for iPhone and Android before release

The phone app connects to Ovis on the computer where your coding agents run. Set up the computer first, then join the test for your phone.

On your computer

First install Tailscale on the computer and on your phone, and sign in to both with the same account. It lets the phone reach the computer wherever you are. On a Mac and on Linux, the Ovis installer also sets up the phone's address when it finds Tailscale. Then install Ovis:

  • Mac (macOS 13 or later): download Ovis for Mac, open it and click Install.
  • Windows: install Git for Windows, then run this in PowerShell:
    irm https://ovis.sh/install.ps1 | iex
  • Linux: run this in a terminal:
    curl -fsSL https://ovis.sh/install.sh | sh

On iPhone

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. On the iPhone, open the Ovis beta invitation, tap Accept, then Install.

If TestFlight says this beta is not accepting testers, it is closed to new testers for now: Apple may still be reviewing it, or it may be full. Try again later, or tell us in an issue.

iPhone: the link to join the TestFlight test appears here as soon as it opens.

On Android

The Android app is in a closed test on Google Play, open to the members of the Ovis testers group.

  1. Join the Ovis testers group with the Google account you use on the phone.
  2. With the same account, opt in at play.google.com/apps/testing/sh.ovis.app.
  3. Install Ovis from Google Play. Play can take a few minutes to offer it after you opt in.

Android: the link to join the Google Play test appears here as soon as it opens.

Connect your phone

Open Ovis on the phone and scan the QR code that Ovis on your computer shows. Ovis for Mac shows it in its window; otherwise open Ovis in the computer's browser (normally http://127.0.0.1:7317) and find it under Settings → Phone & devices. If it shows a tailscale serve command instead of a QR code (on Windows, for example), run that command on the computer, then look again. You can also type the address shown under it. If the phone cannot reach the computer, see Support.

Feedback

Found a bug, or something unclear? Open an issue on GitHub with a screenshot and your device model, for example iPhone 15 or Pixel 8.
